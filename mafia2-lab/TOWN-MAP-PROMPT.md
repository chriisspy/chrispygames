# MAFIA 2 town map: prompt

Top-down pixel-art village at night, 24px tiles, about 24 x 32 tiles (taller than wide), seen from straight above
like a cosy farming game (our own art, not copied from any game). Cool moonlit blues on the grass, warm
orange-yellow light from lamps and windows. Chunky pixels, dark outlines, 2–3 tone shading, no blur.

**Layout (a Town of Salem-style ring):**
- **Centre:** a large cobblestone plaza in a plus/cross shape. In its middle, a round raised wooden stage
  (plank floor, darker rim) where the town votes.
- **Ring of cabins:** 10 small wooden cabins all the way round the plaza: 1 at the top centre, 1 at the
  bottom centre, 4 down the left side and 4 down the right side. Every cabin faces the viewer straight on:
  pitched roof, little brick chimney, front door with a step, two windows glowing warm yellow, a wooden
  porch base. Each cabin has its own colour: red roof, blue, teal-green, purple, orange, cream, dark brown,
  forest green, plum and rust.
- **Paths:** a short cobblestone path runs from every cabin's front step into the plaza.

**Lights:**
- 4 iron street lamps with warm glowing lanterns at the plaza's left and right edges.
- 2 pairs of tall wooden posts, one above and one below the stage, with a string of round glowing bulbs
  swagged between them.
- Soft warm pools of light on the cobbles under each lamp; the rest of the town in blue moonlight.

**Nature:**
- Dark pine trees all round the outside edge, framing the village; a few inside between cabins.
- Round dark-green bushes and small shrubs scattered on the grass.
- A few small dark-blue ponds/puddles on the grass between the cabins.
- Little flower patches and grass tufts; a low wooden fence along the very top edge.

**Mood:** quiet, safe-looking but a little eerie: the kind of town where something happens at night.
